求下述算式的定积分

∫01x−1ln⁡xdx\int_{0}^{1}\frac{x-1}{\ln{x}}dx ∫01​lnxx−1​dx

答案（点击展开）

∫01x−1ln⁡xdx=∫01x1−10ln⁡xdx=ln⁡1+10+1=ln⁡2\begin{aligned} \int_{0}^{1}\frac{x-1}{\ln{x}}dx &= \int_{0}^{1}\frac{x^1-1^0}{\ln{x}}dx \\ &= \ln{\frac{1+1}{0+1}} \\ &= \ln{2} \end{aligned} ∫01​lnxx−1​dx​=∫01​lnxx1−10​dx=ln0+11+1​=ln2​

拓展问题

∫01xa−xbln⁡xdx\int_{0}^{1}\frac{x^a-x^b}{\ln{x}}dx ∫01​lnxxa−xb​dx

详解（点击展开）

方法1：转化成二重积分

∫01xa−xbln⁡xdx=∫01(xbln⁡x−xaln⁡x)dx=∫01(∫abxydy)dx=∫abdy∫01xydx=∫ab1y+1dy=ln⁡(y+1)∣ab=ln⁡b+1a+1\begin{aligned} \int_{0}^{1}\frac{x^a-x^b}{\ln{x}}dx &= \int_{0}^{1}\left(\frac{x^b}{\ln{x}}-\frac{x^a}{\ln{x}}\right)dx \\ &= \int_{0}^{1}\left(\int_{a}^{b}x^y dy\right)dx \\ &= \int_{a}^{b}dy\int_{0}^{1}x^y dx \\ &= \int_{a}^{b}\frac{1}{y+1}dy \\ &= \left.\ln(y+1)\right|^b_a \\ &= \ln\frac{b+1}{a+1} \end{aligned} ∫01​lnxxa−xb​dx​=∫01​(lnxxb​−lnxxa​)dx=∫01​(∫ab​xydy)dx=∫ab​dy∫01​xydx=∫ab​y+11​dy=ln(y+1)∣ab​=lna+1b+1​​

方法2：费曼积分法

令

f(a,b)=∫01xb−xaln⁡xdxf(a,b)=\int_{0}^{1}\frac{x^b-x^a}{\ln{x}}dx f(a,b)=∫01​lnxxb−xa​dx

对参数求导：

f′(a,b)=∫01(xb−xa)dx=1b+1−1a+1f'(a,b) = \int_{0}^{1}(x^b-x^a)dx = \frac{1}{b+1}-\frac{1}{a+1} f′(a,b)=∫01​(xb−xa)dx=b+11​−a+11​

积分得：

f(a,b)=ln⁡b+1a+1+Cf(a,b) = \ln\frac{b+1}{a+1} + C f(a,b)=lna+1b+1​+C

由边界条件 f(0,0)=0f(0,0)=0f(0,0)=0 得 C=0C=0C=0，故：

∫01xa−xbln⁡xdx=ln⁡b+1a+1\int_{0}^{1}\frac{x^a-x^b}{\ln{x}}dx = \ln\frac{b+1}{a+1} ∫01​lnxxa−xb​dx=lna+1b+1​
